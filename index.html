📖 How to Use
  1. Setup Microphone: Click "Setup Microphone" and grant permission. Select your preferred input device.
  2. Capture Tab Audio: Click "Capture Tab/Screen Audio". Select the tab with your meeting (Teams, Zoom, etc.) and make sure to check "Share tab audio".
  3. Start Recording: Once both sources show green status, click "Start Recording".
  4. Stop & Download: Click "Stop Recording" when done. Download both audio files separately.
  5. Transcribe: Use FasterWhisperXXL or similar tool with the separate audio files for better transcription accuracy.

Tips for Best Results:

  • Use Chrome or Edge browser for best compatibility
  • When capturing tab audio, select "Chrome Tab" option (not window/screen)
  • Always check the "Share tab audio" checkbox when prompted
  • Keep this tab open in the background while recording
  • Use headphones to prevent echo between your mic and speakers
